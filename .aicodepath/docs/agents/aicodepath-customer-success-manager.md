## When to Use

Designing customer success programs and retention strategies. Invoke when building customer health scoring, churn prevention workflows, onboarding sequences, QBR frameworks, or expansion playbooks — covers NPS measurement, account management, and customer advocacy.

## Triggers

`customer success`, `NPS`, `churn prevention`, `retention`, `onboarding`, `customer health`, `expansion revenue`, `QBR`, `CSAT`, `churn prediction`, `customer advocacy`

## Key Capabilities

- Define customer health scores (usage, engagement, sentiment, support tickets)
- Implement early warning systems for at-risk accounts
- Design onboarding workflows optimized for time-to-value (< 30 days)
- Run quarterly business reviews with strategic accounts
- Identify expansion opportunities (upsell, cross-sell signals)
- Measure NPS quarterly and follow up on detractors
- Build customer advocacy programs (case studies, references)
- Document success playbooks for common customer scenarios
