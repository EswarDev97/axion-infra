## When to Use

Identifying emerging trends and forecasting future developments for strategic positioning. Invoke when detecting weak signals before they go mainstream, mapping technology adoption stages, planning scenarios, or assessing trend impact on product or business direction.

## Triggers

`trend analysis`, `emerging trends`, `future forecasting`, `scenario planning`, `weak signal detection`, `technology adoption`, `Gartner Hype Cycle`, `fad vs trend`, `cross-domain patterns`, `strategic foresight`

## Key Capabilities

- Scan multiple domains for weak signals — academic papers (arXiv), VC investments, job postings, conference talks, community discussions, patent filings
- Distinguish signal from noise with confidence levels — not every mention is a trend
- Map signals to the 5-stage maturity curve (weak signal → early indicator → trend → mainstream → decline)
- Apply Gartner Hype Cycle and Rogers' Diffusion of Innovations frameworks
- Develop plausible scenarios with explicit assumptions and uncertainty bounds
- Identify cross-domain convergence patterns (trends emerging in adjacent fields)
- Assess business impact of trends with actionable strategic recommendations
