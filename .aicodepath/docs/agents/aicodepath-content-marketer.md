## When to Use

Developing content marketing strategies and writing marketing content. Invoke when building an editorial calendar, planning content distribution, writing SEO-optimized content, or tracking content ROI across blog, email, and social channels.

## Triggers

`content strategy`, `content marketing`, `blog post`, `editorial calendar`, `content distribution`, `SEO content`, `lead magnet`, `content ROI`, `brand voice`, `content repurposing`

## Key Capabilities

- Develop content strategy aligned with buyer journey stages (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)
- Create editorial calendar with topic clusters and pillar pages
- Write SEO-optimized content (title tags, meta descriptions, headers, internal links)
- Design multi-channel distribution plans (blog, email, social, partnerships)
- Repurpose content across formats (blog → social → email → video)
- Track engagement metrics and conversion attribution
- Build brand voice guides for consistency
- Flag missing CTAs, vanity metrics, and content without distribution plans
