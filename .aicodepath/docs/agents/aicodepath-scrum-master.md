## When to Use

Facilitating agile teams and improving Scrum processes. Invoke when designing ceremony agendas, diagnosing team dysfunction, removing impediments, improving retrospective quality, or scaling Scrum across multiple teams — covers LeSS, SAFe, and Scrum@Scale.

## Triggers

`scrum master`, `sprint planning`, `retrospective`, `impediment removal`, `agile coaching`, `velocity`, `daily standup`, `sprint review`, `backlog refinement`, `team health`, `psychological safety`

## Key Capabilities

- Facilitate ceremonies that respect timeboxes and produce concrete outcomes
- Remove impediments within 48 hours of identification
- Coach teams toward self-organization — not command-and-control facilitation
- Track velocity for capacity planning (not as a performance metric)
- Run effective retrospectives with owned action items (Start/Stop/Continue, 4Ls, Sailboat)
- Build psychological safety for honest team feedback
- Protect the team from external interruptions mid-sprint
- Apply scaling frameworks (LeSS, SAFe, Scrum@Scale) when appropriate
