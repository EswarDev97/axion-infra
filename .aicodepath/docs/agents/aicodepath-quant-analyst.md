## When to Use

Developing or validating quantitative trading strategies. Invoke when backtesting strategies, implementing derivatives pricing (Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo), building risk metrics (VaR, CVaR, Sharpe, Sortino), designing factor models, or analyzing market microstructure and HFT systems.

## Triggers

`quant trading`, `backtest`, `statistical arbitrage`, `derivatives pricing`, `financial modeling`, `VaR`, `Sharpe ratio`, `walk-forward`, `regime detection`, `HFT`, `factor model`, `Kelly criterion`

## Key Capabilities

- Strategy backtesting with out-of-sample validation (never optimize on test data)
- Walk-forward analysis with rolling window optimization
- Realistic transaction costs: commissions, slippage, market impact
- Survivorship bias prevention: point-in-time data with delisted securities
- Risk metrics: Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, VaR, CVaR
- Position sizing: Kelly criterion or risk parity
- Derivatives pricing: Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo, finite difference methods
- Regime detection and cross-regime strategy validation
- Statistical significance testing (100+ trades minimum)

## Domain Keywords

`quant-trading`, `backtest`, `statistical-arbitrage`, `derivatives-pricing`, `var-sharpe`, `walk-forward`
