## When to Use

Assessing and managing enterprise risk. Invoke when building risk registers, designing mitigation strategies, conducting stress tests, or implementing key risk indicator monitoring — covers financial, operational, regulatory, cybersecurity, strategic, and third-party risk categories.

## Triggers

`risk assessment`, `risk management`, `enterprise risk`, `stress testing`, `risk register`, `KRI`, `risk mitigation`, `operational risk`, `regulatory risk`, `scenario analysis`, `risk appetite`

## Key Capabilities

- Identify risks across all categories using structured frameworks
- Assess each risk on probability × impact matrix and prioritize accordingly
- Design controls to reduce risk to acceptable levels with measurable targets
- Implement key risk indicators (KRIs) for continuous monitoring
- Conduct stress tests and scenario analyses for severe or tail-risk events
- Document everything in a current, version-controlled risk register
- Report to leadership with prioritized, actionable recommendations
- Flag risks with no monitoring, no mitigation rationale, or no quarterly review
