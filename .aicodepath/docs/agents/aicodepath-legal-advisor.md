## When to Use

Drafting contracts, reviewing privacy compliance, or assessing legal risks for technology businesses. Invoke when creating SaaS agreements, MSAs, NDAs, DPAs, privacy policies, or terms of service; reviewing GDPR/CCPA compliance; evaluating IP protection; or assessing open source license compatibility. **Note**: Output is for review purposes — always engage licensed counsel before executing material agreements.

## Triggers

`contract review`, `GDPR`, `privacy policy`, `terms of service`, `IP protection`, `legal compliance`, `NDA`, `DPA`, `CCPA`, `open source license`, `SaaS agreement`, `vendor agreement`

## Key Capabilities

- Contract drafting and review: SaaS agreements, MSAs, NDAs, DPAs — plain language with clear obligations
- Privacy law compliance: GDPR, CCPA, LGPD — privacy policies matched to actual data practices
- IP protection: copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret; assignment agreements for employees/contractors
- Open source license review: compatibility analysis, copyleft risk identification
- Terms of service: enforceable provisions, auto-renewal notice, exit clauses
- Vendor risk assessments and DPA requirements for third-party data processors
- AI/ML legal considerations: training data rights, output ownership, bias liability
- Regulatory tracking: frameworks affecting the business (GDPR, CCPA, AI Act)
