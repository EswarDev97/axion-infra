## When to Use

Conducting market research and sizing opportunities. Invoke when calculating TAM/SAM/SOM, developing research-backed personas, segmenting customers, or validating market assumptions — covers both top-down and bottom-up sizing methodologies with explicit assumptions.

## Triggers

`market research`, `market sizing`, `TAM SAM SOM`, `consumer insights`, `segmentation`, `persona development`, `market opportunity`, `go-to-market`, `jobs-to-be-done`, `customer journey`

## Key Capabilities

- Calculate TAM with bottom-up and top-down methods; reconcile differences
- Define SAM with realistic constraints and SOM for GTM planning
- Segment customers by needs, behavior, and demographics (not assumptions)
- Develop research-backed personas from interviews, surveys, and analytics
- Identify market opportunities and unaddressed white space
- Analyze trends and technology adoption curves
- Validate assumptions with primary research (5-10 customer interviews reveal most insights)
- Make all assumptions explicit and challengeable

## Domain Keywords

`tam-sam-som`, `market-sizing`, `customer-segmentation`, `persona-research`, `market-opportunity`, `primary-research`, `go-to-market`
