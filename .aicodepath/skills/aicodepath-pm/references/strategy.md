# Product Strategy — Templates & Frameworks

## Product Strategy Canvas (9 sections)

```
## Product Strategy — [Product Name]

### 1. Vision
[Inspiring, 1-sentence future state — not a feature, a world]

### 2. Target Customer
[Specific segment — not "everyone". Who is the beachhead?]

### 3. Customer Problem
[The burning problem — in customer language, not product language]

### 4. Unique Value Proposition
[What you do that no one else does, or does better]

### 5. Solution
[High-level approach — what category of solution, not feature list]

### 6. Business Model
[How you make money — revenue streams + cost structure summary]

### 7. Moat / Defensibility
[Network effects / data / switching costs / brand / IP / distribution]

### 8. Key Metrics
[2-3 metrics that prove the strategy is working]

### 9. Strategic Bets
[The 1-3 assumptions this strategy depends on being true]
```

---

## Product Vision Statement

Formula (inspired by Geoffrey Moore):
> "For [target customer] who [has this problem], [product name] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], our product [differentiator]."

Write 2-3 versions, each with a different emotional tone (aspirational / practical / disruptive).

## Value Proposition (JTBD Template)

```
WHO — [Target customer segment, specific]
WHY — [Functional + emotional job they're trying to do]
WHAT BEFORE — [Current pain / frustration / workaround]
HOW — [What your product does, specifically]
WHAT AFTER — [Outcome they experience — measurable if possible]
ALTERNATIVES — [What they'd use if you didn't exist]
```

## Business Model Canvas (9 building blocks)

| Block | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Customer Segments** | Who are you creating value for? |
| **Value Propositions** | What value do you deliver? |
| **Channels** | How do you reach segments? |
| **Customer Relationships** | What relationship does each segment expect? |
| **Revenue Streams** | What are customers willing to pay for? |
| **Key Resources** | What resources does your value prop require? |
| **Key Activities** | What activities does your value prop require? |
| **Key Partnerships** | Who are your key partners/suppliers? |
| **Cost Structure** | What are the most important costs? |

```
- [S2]

- [W2]

- [O1]
- [O2]

### Threats (external, negative)
- [T1]
- [T2]

### Strategic Recommendations
- SO (leverage strengths to capture opportunities): [action]
- WO (overcome weaknesses by pursuing opportunities): [action]
- ST (use strengths to mitigate threats): [action]
- WT (minimize weaknesses, avoid threats): [action]
```
