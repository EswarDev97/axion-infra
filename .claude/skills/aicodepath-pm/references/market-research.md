## Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)

Use both approaches for `$ARGUMENTS`:

**Top-Down:**
```
Total addressable market (TAM): $[X]B
Source: [Report/estimate]
Serviceable addressable market (SAM): $[X]M
[How you narrowed: geography / segment / use case]
Serviceable obtainable market (SOM): $[X]M
[Realistic 3-5 year capture based on team/budget/competition]
```

**Bottom-Up:**
```
Target customers: [N companies/users]
× Average contract value / ARPU: $[X]
= SAM: $[X]M

Realistic win rate in year 1-3: [%]
= SOM: $[X]M
```

**Validation:** Do both approaches converge? If they diverge >3×, explain why.
